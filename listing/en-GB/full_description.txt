DropDroid moves files from your computer to your Android phone directly over your own local network. Nothing is uploaded to the internet, a cloud drive or any server. There is no account to create.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open DropDroid on your phone.
2. Start the free DropDroid desktop portal on your computer (Windows, macOS or Linux, from github.com/tjdam007/DropDroid).
3. Scan the QR code shown in the portal to pair.
4. Drop any file into the portal. It arrives on your phone in seconds.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Local only: files travel straight between your devices over Wi-Fi, phone hotspot, LAN or USB tethering.
• Secure pairing: only the portal you scanned can send files to your phone.
• Every transfer is signed and its contents are verified, so altered or repeated uploads are rejected.
• No ads, no accounts, no sign-in.

BUILT FOR EVERYDAY USE
• Any file type: photos, videos, PDFs, documents, archives and more.
• Recent files list: tap a file to open it in the right app.
• Choose your save folder, or keep the default Downloads / DropDroid folder.
• Receives in the background with a status notification showing progress.
• Automatic device discovery, with a manual IP fallback for strict networks.
• Light and dark themes, and a two-pane layout on tablets and foldables.

FOR DEVELOPERS AND TESTERS
Sending an APK? Turn on the optional install helper and DropDroid opens Android's installer once the file arrives. Android always asks you to confirm before anything is installed.

OPEN SOURCE
DropDroid is free and open source under the MIT licence. Read the code, report issues or contribute at github.com/tjdam007/DropDroid.

Note: DropDroid receives files. You need the DropDroid desktop portal running on a computer that can reach your phone on the same local network.
